How these counts work

Appointments count a person’s role on a journal. One person with roles on two journals contributes two appointments. Overlapping list records are combined where the same appointment matches. Different roles or affiliations can remain separate rows.

Editors are distinct normalized names, not verified person identities. Identical names can merge; different spellings can count separately. Editor totals across journals or institutions should not be added together.

What changes with filters? Filters select journal rows. Each row’s appointment, editor, and country counts remain totals across all lists for that journal; the coverage cards also cover all lists. View editors keeps your selected lists; without a list selection, it opens the relevant results across all lists.

Scope. This table includes journals with Directory board data. A journal in Rankings may have no board here. Countries follow recorded affiliations, not editors’ nationalities.

Lists and grades. FT50, Top 5, and UTD24 indicate list membership. ABDC A*–C are 2025 Journal Quality List grades; AJG 4*–1 are 2024 CABS Academic Journal Guide grades. These are separate systems, not equivalent scores. AJG-gap adds boards for journals outside the FT50 and ABDC editor files.

Coverage. Collection dates describe the saved board releases across all lists, not a live check of every journal. Individual boards can be older, including the archived African Affairs board. Missing grades or affiliations mean information is unavailable, not zero.

About this data

About this journal list

Each row is one journal in the editor directory. Appointment, editor, and country counts are taken from those collapsed rows. An editor who sits on two journals is counted once in the header and once on each journal. List badges are FT50, ABDC A*, A, B, or C, plus Top 5 and UTD24 when the journal is on those lists. An AJG badge (AJG 4*, AJG 4, AJG 3, AJG 2, or AJG 1) appears when that journal carries a 2024 CABS grade. Many ABDC journals have none. AJG-gap journals carry that grade and are not given an FT50 or ABDC badge.

Field is field_for from the ABDC journal catalogs, joined on the catalog id when the release has one. Journals without a field stay in the table. The board link is the masthead URL on the release, or the catalog board URL when that cell is empty. Emails, phone numbers, and photos are not published.

Board year 2026. Institutions are on the Institutions page. The AJG badge, when the journal has one, is its grade in the 2024 CABS Academic Journal Guide. On FT50 and ABDC journals the grade is joined by catalog id. On AJG-gap journals it comes from the panel in data/editors/abs_ajg_gap/releases/current/. The guide has 1,823 journals. Directory boards are the overlap with the FT50 and ABDC releases, plus the AJG-gap freeze, not the whole guide. Earlier editions, and titles with no board, stay on Rankings.

Build locally with python scripts/build_site.py. The project site is kochiuyu.github.io/econ-journal-dispersion.

Coverage and scope

One row per journal on the current FT50 and ABDC boards, plus AJG-gap boards for journals that are not already on those lists. Top 5 and UTD24 stay badges on the FT50 and ABDC journals. An AJG chip is a 2024 CABS grade (AJG 4*, AJG 4, AJG 3, AJG 2, or AJG 1) on a journal that has one. Many ABDC journals have no grade. It does not add the rest of the guide. View editors opens the directory filtered to that journal and your selected lists.

Using the filters

Selecting more than one list shows journals on any of them. An AJG chip is the 2024 CABS grade on a journal in this directory, including AJG-gap boards. It does not invent an FT50 or ABDC badge for a gap journal. Field is the ABDC field of research when the catalog has one. AJG-gap journals often have no ABDC field. Board opens the masthead page when the release has a public URL. View editors opens the directory on that journal and your selected lists.
