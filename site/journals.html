About this journal list

Each row is one journal in the editor directory. Appointment, editor, and country counts are taken from those collapsed rows. An editor who sits on two journals is counted once in the header and once on each journal. List badges are FT50, ABDC A*, A, B, or C, plus Top 5 and UTD24 when the journal is on those lists.

Field is field_for from the ABDC journal catalogs, joined on the catalog id when the release has one. Journals without a field stay in the table. The board link is the masthead URL on the release, or the catalog board URL when that cell is empty. Emails, phone numbers, and photos are not published.

Board year 2026. Institutions are on the Institutions page. Journal grades and membership lists are on Rankings and are not list chips here.

Build locally with python scripts/build_site.py. The project site is kochiuyu.github.io/econ-journal-dispersion.
