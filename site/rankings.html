About these rankings

This page lists journal grades and membership lists. It is separate from the editor directory. The editor directory is FT50, ABDC A*/A/B/C, and AJG-gap boards. Top 5 and UTD24 filter that directory as tags on the FT50 and ABDC boards. Directory AJG chips are the 2024 grade (AJG 4*, AJG 4, AJG 3, AJG 2, AJG 1), joined by catalog id from this freeze, including crosswalk ids, onto FT50 and ABDC boards. A chip from that join is a grade on a board journal. Unmatched titles on those boards are not tagged by the join. AJG-gap boards are journals not already in those editor files; their grade comes from the panel. This page still lists every ABS/AJG edition and grade, including titles with no board.

ABS/AJG is the CABS Academic Journal Guide in the Editor-Collector freeze rankings_abs_ajg_20261024. Editions are 2010, 2015, 2018, 2021, and 2024. Ratings are 4*, 4, 3, 2, and 1. ABS 2010 has no 4*. AJG 2024 has 1,823 journals, of which 1,378 (75.6%) have a catalog journal_id. About 1,356 of those mapped titles are on a current FT50 or ABDC board and can show a Directory chip from the id join. The 445 with no id are not tagged by that join, and a small set of mapped titles are not on those boards. Many ABDC board journals have no AJG grade (about 2,400 board journal ids, against about 1,356 with a hit). AJG-gap boards add journals that are not in those editor files. Directory chips cover 1,796 journals. 27 titles still have no board. Across all five editions, 1,478 of 7,245 rows are unmatched. The files are under journals/rankings/abs_ajg/releases/rankings_abs_ajg_20261024/.

CNRS is Section 37 Catégorisation des revues in freeze rankings_cnrs_20261024. Editions are 2011–2015 and 2017–2020 (no separate 2016 list). Ratings are 1, 1e, 1g, 1eg, 2, 3, and 4, as published. Field is the domain code on that freeze. June 2020 has 840 journals, of which 713 (84.9%) have a catalog journal_id. The long panel has 7,361 rows.

Handelsblatt/VWL is Forschungsmonitoring journal weights plus the 2015 Handelsblatt list, in freeze rankings_handelsblatt_vwl_20261024. Editions are 1998–2020. The weight is the published continuous value. Letter classes are A+, A, B, C, D, E, and F when the freeze has one. The 2015 Handelsblatt PDF rows have a weight and no letter class. 2020 has 1,777 journals, of which 882 (49.6%) have a catalog journal_id. 2015 has 3,384 rows because it stacks both sources. The long panel has 42,478 rows. This list has no field.

Tilburg is the TISEM top journal list in freeze rankings_tilburg_20261025, from the Tilburg University page. The page does not name a dated edition, so this list is Current. Tiers are Top-core, Top, and Very good. The page intro mentions good journals, and the page does not publish a good-journal list. Top-core has 42 journals, including Journal of Economic Literature and Journal of Economic Perspectives from the allocation supplement. Top has 41. Very good has 96. 164 of 179 journals (91.6%) have a catalog journal_id. Very good rows use the group on that page as the field. The other tiers have no field. The label on this page is Tilburg. The grade hover says TISEM.

FNEGE is the Journal Quality List in freeze rankings_fnege_20261025, from the FNEGE table. Editions are 2019, 2022, and 2025. 2025 is the current list: 891 journals, of which 733 (82.3%) have a catalog journal_id. 2022 has 669 journals (577 matched, 86.2%). 2019 has 484 (410 matched, 84.7%). Grades are 1*, 1, 2, 3, and 4. The table also prints EM and NC; those cells are kept as published. 2025 has 31 journals at 1*, 59 at 1, 153 at 2, 251 at 3, 393 at 4, and 4 EM. The long panel has 2,044 rows, of which 1,720 (84.1%) are matched. Field is the published domain code.

ERIM is the ERIM Journals List Core for 2024–2028 in freeze rankings_erim_20261025. The public page is the ERIM Journal List, and the rows are the Core workbook. The intranet list is not included. Classes are the Core file’s own labels: STAR 44, M-STAR 3, P 69, EP* 3, PA 5, S 144, S (3=1) 7, and M 8. 257 of 283 journals (90.8%) have a catalog journal_id. Field is the Core track.

Stern is the NYU Stern Economics top-tier journal memo of 31 July 2020 in freeze rankings_stern_20261025. The department page is Top-Tier Journals by Department, and the rows are the economics memo. The finance list on that page is not included. Tiers are Top 5 (5), Really excellent (10 general-interest journals), Field (27), and Review (7). 47 of 49 journals (95.9%) have a catalog journal_id. Field journals use the memo’s field heading. The other tiers have no field. The label on this page is Stern.

Shanghai is the ShanghaiRanking top-journal list for Economics, Business Administration, Finance, and Management in freeze rankings_shanghai_20261025. The survey page is the Academic Excellence Survey. 2026 is the 2026 PDF: 19 journals, all with a catalog journal_id. Economics is the classic top five and is kept as a subject. 2024 is the 2024 PDF: 57 journals, of which 55 (96.5%) have a catalog journal_id. Membership is binary, so every row is Top. Field is the subject. A journal in two subjects is one row, and Field joins those subjects. 2026 is 19 journals because Academy of Management Journal, Administrative Science Quarterly, and Strategic Management Journal are in both Business Administration and Management. 2024 Business Administration is the Journal Citation Report block in that PDF. The other included rows are the Academic Excellence Survey. The long panel has 76 rows, of which 74 (97.4%) are matched. The label on this page is Shanghai.

ABDC is the 2025 ABDC Journal Quality List, one edition, with ratings A*, A, B, and C. Those tiers are the catalogs journals/catalog_abdc_a_star.csv (219), catalog_abdc_a.csv (630), catalog_abdc_b.csv (861), and catalog_abdc_c.csv (941): 2,651 journals.

FT50, UTD24, and Top 5 are membership lists. The catalogs have no grade column, so this page does not assign one. FT50 is the April 2026 list in journals/catalog_ft50.csv (50 journals). UTD24 is journals/catalog_utd24.csv (24 journals). Top 5 is journals/catalog_top5.csv (5 journals).

The Field column is the source subject. ABS/AJG uses the guide’s field code. CNRS uses its domain code. ABDC uses field_for from the 2025 catalogs. FNEGE uses its domain code. ERIM uses the Core track. Tilburg very-good rows use the group on the TISEM page. Stern field journals use the memo’s field heading. Shanghai uses the subject, and joins two subjects on one row. Handelsblatt/VWL, FT50, UTD24, and Top 5 have no field, so those cells stay blank.

On ABS/AJG, CNRS, Handelsblatt/VWL, Tilburg, FNEGE, ERIM, Stern, and Shanghai, journal_id is the freeze’s primary catalog id. A blank cell is an unmatched title, listed in unmatched_journals.csv. On ABDC, FT50, UTD24, and Top 5, journal_id is the catalog id.
