About these rankings

This page lists journal grades and membership lists. It is separate from the editor directory. FT50, ABDC, Top 5, UTD24, and AJG grades filter the editor directory. The directory AJG chips are the 2024 grade (AJG 4*, AJG 4, AJG 3, AJG 2, AJG 1), joined by catalog id from this freeze, including crosswalk ids. This page still lists every ABS/AJG edition and grade.

ABS/AJG is the CABS Academic Journal Guide in the Editor-Collector freeze rankings_abs_ajg_20261024. Editions are 2010, 2015, 2018, 2021, and 2024. Ratings are 4*, 4, 3, 2, and 1. ABS 2010 has no 4*. AJG 2024 has 1,823 journals, of which 1,378 (75.6%) have a catalog journal_id. Across all five editions, 1,478 of 7,245 rows are unmatched. The files are under journals/rankings/abs_ajg/releases/rankings_abs_ajg_20261024/.

CNRS is Section 37 Catégorisation des revues in freeze rankings_cnrs_20261024. Editions are 2011–2015 and 2017–2020 (no separate 2016 list). Ratings are 1, 1e, 1g, 1eg, 2, 3, and 4, as published. Field is the domain code on that freeze. June 2020 has 840 journals, of which 713 (84.9%) have a catalog journal_id. The long panel has 7,361 rows.

Handelsblatt/VWL is Forschungsmonitoring journal weights plus the 2015 Handelsblatt list, in freeze rankings_handelsblatt_vwl_20261024. Editions are 1998–2020. The weight is the published continuous value. Letter classes are A+, A, B, C, D, E, and F when the freeze has one. The 2015 Handelsblatt PDF rows have a weight and no letter class. 2020 has 1,777 journals, of which 882 (49.6%) have a catalog journal_id. 2015 has 3,384 rows because it stacks both sources. The long panel has 42,478 rows. This list has no field.

ABDC is the 2025 ABDC Journal Quality List, one edition, with ratings A*, A, B, and C. Those tiers are the catalogs journals/catalog_abdc_a_star.csv (219), catalog_abdc_a.csv (630), catalog_abdc_b.csv (861), and catalog_abdc_c.csv (941): 2,651 journals.

FT50, UTD24, and Top 5 are membership lists. The catalogs have no grade column, so this page does not assign one. FT50 is the April 2026 list in journals/catalog_ft50.csv (50 journals). UTD24 is journals/catalog_utd24.csv (24 journals). Top 5 is journals/catalog_top5.csv (5 journals).

The Field column is the source subject. ABS/AJG uses the guide’s field code. CNRS uses its domain code. ABDC uses field_for from the 2025 catalogs. Handelsblatt/VWL, FT50, UTD24, and Top 5 have no field, so those cells stay blank.

On ABS/AJG, journal_id is the freeze’s primary catalog id. A blank cell is an unmatched title, listed in unmatched_journals.csv. On ABDC, FT50, UTD24, and Top 5, journal_id is the catalog id.
