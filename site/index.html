About this directory

This is the public editor list for Geographic Dispersion / econ-journal-dispersion. It is built from the analysis releases under data/editors/*/releases/current/, not from a live scrape of this repository. The FT50 file is the ROR-enriched current board. ABDC is the 2025 Journal Quality List at A*, A, B, and C. Top 5, UTD24, and AJG 2024 are tags on those releases. Top 5 and UTD24 are read from journals/catalog_top5.csv and journals/catalog_utd24.csv. AJG 2024 is read from journals/rankings/abs_ajg/releases/rankings_abs_ajg_20261024/. core49 is an older ABDC A* subset and is not a separate list here. Authors are not in this version.

Board year 2026. Counts above are appointments (one person, in one role, on one journal), distinct names, journals, and countries with a matched institution. Two people whose names fold to the same string are counted once. Different spellings are counted twice.

How overlap is shown

Where the same person, journal, and role appear in both the FT50 release and an ABDC rating, the table shows one row and both badges. The wording comes from the FT50 file. A missing affiliation or country is filled from the ABDC row. A different role, or the same role on another journal, stays a separate row. The same name and role on one journal with two different affiliations is kept as two rows. The role filter groups masthead titles (Editor-in-Chief, Editor, Co-Editor, Senior Editor, Managing Editor, Associate Editor, Guest / Special Issue Editor, Editorial Board, Review Board, Advisory Board, and Other). The Role column is the journal’s own title.

Top 5 and UTD24

Top 5 is American Economic Review, Econometrica, Journal of Political Economy, Quarterly Journal of Economics, and Review of Economic Studies. Each of those appointments also carries the FT50 badge. UTD24 is the UT Dallas Naveen Jindal list of 24 business journals. Twenty-three of them are in the FT50 release. INFORMS Journal on Computing, listed by UTD as Journal on Computing, is included from the ABDC A release so the chip covers the full list. Journal of Applied Psychology remains an FT50 appointment and does not carry a UTD24 badge.

AJG 2024

AJG 2024 is the CABS Academic Journal Guide 2024 edition. The chip means the journal is on that list, at any grade. The id join uses abs_ajg_2024.csv and the 2024 rows of journal_id_crosswalk.csv, so a journal whose primary id is a core49 slug still matches the ABDC id on the board file. The Economic Journal is one of those. Titles the freeze left unmatched are not tagged, and journals that are not on the 2024 list do not get the badge. Accounting Educators Journal stays an ABDC journal without an AJG 2024 badge. Other editions and the grade table stay on the Rankings page.

Institution and field

Institution lists the affiliation stored on the row: the matched ROR name when the release has one, otherwise the canonical or masthead affiliation. Choosing an institution keeps appointments with that exact affiliation. The Institutions page links here with that name. Field is field_for from the 2025 ABDC catalogs, the same subject as the Field filter on Journals. The menu shows the subject name. The code stays on the cell title.

Country and continent are the ROR fields on the release. Affiliations prefer the matched institution name. Emails, phone numbers, and photos are not published; contact strings found in a masthead field are removed. Journal names link to the public board page the row was collected from.

Journals is one row per journal. Institutions is one row per institution, using the ROR id when the release has one. A journal name opens this directory on that journal. An institution name opens it on that affiliation and country.

CNRS and Handelsblatt/VWL grades, the other ABS/AJG editions, the 2025 ABDC ratings, and FT50, UTD24, and Top 5 membership lists are on the Rankings page. Those rows are journal grades, not editorial appointments. The AJG chip on this directory is 2024 membership only.
