Current FT50 and ABDC journal editors

About this directory

This is the public editor list for Geographic Dispersion / econ-journal-dispersion. It is built from the analysis releases under data/editors/*/releases/current/, not from a live scrape of this repository. The FT50 file is the ROR-enriched current board. ABDC is the 2025 Journal Quality List at A*, A, B, and C. Those two releases are the whole directory. Top 5 and UTD24 are tags read from journals/catalog_top5.csv and journals/catalog_utd24.csv. AJG chips are 2024 CABS grades tagged onto the same rows from journals/rankings/abs_ajg/releases/rankings_abs_ajg_20261024/ and its crosswalk. They do not add a board scrape. core49 is an older ABDC A* subset and is not a separate list here. Authors are not in this version.

Board year 2026. Counts above are appointments (one person, in one role, on one journal), distinct names, journals, and countries with a matched institution. Two people whose names fold to the same string are counted once. Different spellings are counted twice.

How overlap is shown

Where the same person, journal, and role appear in both the FT50 release and an ABDC rating, the table shows one row and both badges. The wording comes from the FT50 file. A missing affiliation or country is filled from the ABDC row. A different role, or the same role on another journal, stays a separate row. The same name and role on one journal with two different affiliations is kept as two rows. The role filter groups masthead titles (Editor-in-Chief, Editor, Co-Editor, Senior Editor, Managing Editor, Associate Editor, Guest / Special Issue Editor, Editorial Board, Review Board, Advisory Board, and Other). The Role column is the journal’s own title.

Top 5 and UTD24

Top 5 is American Economic Review, Econometrica, Journal of Political Economy, Quarterly Journal of Economics, and Review of Economic Studies. Each of those appointments also carries the FT50 badge. UTD24 is the UT Dallas Naveen Jindal list of 24 business journals. Twenty-three of them are in the FT50 release. INFORMS Journal on Computing, listed by UTD as Journal on Computing, is included from the ABDC A release so the chip covers the full list. Journal of Applied Psychology remains an FT50 appointment and does not carry a UTD24 badge.

AJG grades

AJG chips are grades on the FT50 and ABDC rows. The list chip and the row badge both show one grade: AJG 4*, AJG 4, AJG 3, AJG 2, or AJG 1. The grade is rating on the 2024 edition, the latest edition in journals/rankings/abs_ajg/releases/rankings_abs_ajg_20261024/. The id join uses abs_ajg_2024.csv and the 2024 rows of journal_id_crosswalk.csv, so a journal whose primary id is a core49 slug still matches the ABDC id on the board file. The Economic Journal is one of those, and its badge is AJG 4.

That edition has 1,823 journals. 1,378 map to a catalog id, and about 1,356 of those sit on a current board and can show a chip here. The 445 unmatched titles have no id and never get a chip. A small set of mapped titles are not on these boards, so they stay off the directory too. Many ABDC board journals have no AJG grade: about 2,400 ABDC board journal ids, against about 1,356 with an AJG hit. Accounting Educators Journal stays an ABDC journal without an AJG badge. Earlier editions, and the AJG titles that never receive a chip, stay on the Rankings page. ABDC labels are unchanged.

Institution and field

Institution lists the affiliation stored on the row: the matched ROR name when the release has one, otherwise the canonical or masthead affiliation. Choosing an institution keeps appointments with that exact affiliation. The Institutions page links here with that name. Field is field_for from the 2025 ABDC catalogs, the same subject as the Field filter on Journals. The menu shows the subject name. The code stays on the cell title.

Country and continent are the ROR fields on the release. Affiliations prefer the matched institution name. Emails, phone numbers, and photos are not published; contact strings found in a masthead field are removed. Journal names link to the public board page the row was collected from. A name links to a faculty profile when faculty_profile_url is filled. Coverage is partial: higher on FT50 and ABDC A* than on B and C, so a name with no profile stays plain text. The directory does not link faculty_home_page, and it does not invent ORCID, OpenAlex, or RePEc person links.

Journals is one row per journal. Institutions is one row per institution, using the ROR id when the release has one. A journal name opens this directory on that journal. An institution name opens it on that affiliation and country.

CNRS and Handelsblatt/VWL grades, the earlier ABS/AJG editions, the 2025 ABDC ratings, and FT50, UTD24, and Top 5 membership lists are on the Rankings page. Those rows are journal grades, not editorial appointments. The AJG chips on this directory are the 2024 grades that match a current board.
