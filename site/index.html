About

This site is the public editor list for Geographic Dispersion / econ-journal-dispersion. It is built from the releases under data/editors/*/releases/current/, not from a live scrape. FT50 and the 2025 ABDC list (A*, A, B, and C) are the whole directory. Top 5 and UTD24 are tags. AJG chips are 2024 CABS grades (AJG 4*, AJG 4, AJG 3, AJG 2, and AJG 1) on those rows. core49 is not a separate list. Authors are not included.

Board year 2026. The counts above are every current appointment, not just FT50. The directory downloads FT50 first. Other lists arrive when you select them, or when a journal or institution link needs them. A name opens a faculty profile when the release has one. Emails, phone numbers, and photos are not published.

The notes on overlap, Top 5, UTD24, and AJG grades are on the directory. Full AJG editions stay on Rankings.

Dataset license CC-BY-4.0. Journal mastheads remain the publishers’. Build locally with python scripts/build_site.py. The project site is kochiuyu.github.io/econ-journal-dispersion.
