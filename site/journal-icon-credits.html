Small journal covers and logos help identify titles in Directory, Journals, and Rankings. Images are served from this site. Other titles use initials, which also appear if an image cannot load.

197 journals have verified official images. The latest batch added 50, prioritizing journals with the most Directory appointments. Source records.

FT50 coverage remains 47 of 50 titles. Last expanded on 15 November 2026.

Artwork belongs to the relevant publisher, journal, or other rights holder. No open license or endorsement is implied. Covers identify the journal; they do not represent a ranking edition or the date its editorial board was collected. Raster images were resized to small thumbnails; the American Economic Review SVG was rendered as a thumbnail. Some graphics were extracted from official publisher brochures or graphics packs; those records include the PDF page or archive filename.

The remaining FT50 titles use initials: Journal of Applied Psychology, Journal of Consumer Research, and Quarterly Journal of Economics.
